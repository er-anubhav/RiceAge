Regulatory Certification & Laboratory Limitations Notice
This report provides an optical inspection assessment based on flatbed scan imagery. The following criteria required by Codex Standard for Rice (CXS 198-1995, Revised 2019) cannot be evaluated optically and require dedicated physical laboratory assays:
  • moisture_content (Max 15% m/m - Requires oven/moisture meter)
  • odor (Must be free from abnormal flavors and odors)
  • live_insects (Free from live insects)
This report does not replace certified laboratory grain certificates where legal trade compliance requires moisture or gravimetric weighing.
